Methodology Fixes Over Prototype v1

  • Multiplicative ln(y) modeling: Matches Banff's summer/winter variance scaling.
  • Zero duplicate models: Replaces cloned NBEATSx checkpoints with CatBoost residual, MLForecast LGBM, Ridge+AR(1), and MSTL+ETS.
  • Real Conformal Intervals (80%/95%): Stratified by weekday vs weekend & horizon instead of heuristic min/max model range.
  • Conditional Day-Type Percentiles & Contiguous Windows: Compares weekends fairly to seasonal weekends and validates contiguous calendar spans.
